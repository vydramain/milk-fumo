MILK-FUMO pocket design system

The phone version of the site, as a Pocket PC would show it: one tile fills the screen, the open tiles are tabs at the bottom, and Back closes the current tile. Every screen here is drawn by the site's own pocket.js, the module a touch-first device runs, over the fixtures of the design system (net.js lists them as nginx would), so the page cannot drift from the site. Every screen is live: tap it, drag it.

Open from a static server in the repository root: python3 -m http.server 8000, then localhost:8000/docs/pocket-design-system/. Screens are shown at about 2 CSS pixels per layout pixel. The network is fast; ?net=slow, stalled or offline as in the design system.

Foundations

Palette

The site's palette, unchanged (theme.js): olive surfaces and brass focus for pmc and notes, the navy wells of Catppuccin Mocha for the player and apps.

Fonts

The site's two bitmap fonts at their native size or a whole multiple: rv_font 5×7 for everything, at 2x for a note's first heading and the track name; PxPlus IBM VGA 9×16 at 2x for the playback time.

Why no X in the title bar: phone apps have no close button on a screen; iOS puts Back at the top left and a sheet's close at the top right, Android has the system back. Back at the bottom right is where the thumb is, and the system gesture covers the platform habit.
